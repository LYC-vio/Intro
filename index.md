# Yichen (Henry) Liu
Hi and welcome to my personal website!

I'm a Ph.D. student at [Maizie Zhou's lab](https://lab.vanderbilt.edu/maizie-zhou-lab/), Vanderbilt University. My primary research interest is in computational genomics, with a focus on structural variation, long-read sequencing, and pangenome-based genome inference, with the broader goal of building more accurate and scalable models of human genetic variation.
My research has examined the strengths and limitations of alignment- and assembly-based structural variant calling and translated these insights into new computational methods, including VolcanoSV for diploid SV discovery, DipSVFilter for sequence-consistency-based filtering, and panMARS for population-scale pangenome inference. I also explore alternative sequencing strategies for improving variant detection.
